# Syntracks on TikTok

Screen recordings, no face, English. Every video answers one real question, which song is
playing in a clip people have already seen. Syntracks appears as the tool that answers it,
never as the subject.

## The account

Handle: try `syntracks` first, then `syntracks.app`, then `getsyntracks`. Check availability
while signing up, TikTok returns a page for every name so it cannot be checked from outside.

Bio, 80 characters max:

```text
I find the song. Any TikTok, 30 seconds.
syntracks.app
```

Profile picture is the cassette on yellow, `setup/chrome_extension/icon128.png`.

## The one format

Fifteen seconds, four beats. Do not vary this until forty videos are out.

Fifteen is not a style choice. TikTok now wants roughly seventy percent completion before it
pushes a clip, up from fifty in 2024, and it checks watch time at three, ten and twenty
seconds. A twenty second video has to hold almost everyone to the end. A fifteen second one
does not.

| Time | What is on screen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | The clip everybody knows, sound loud, one line of text on top | Three seconds decide whether the video is shown at all |
| 0:03 to 0:06 | Same clip continues, text asks the question already in the comments | Recognition before curiosity |
| 0:06 to 0:07 | The yellow band opens and the cassette turns | One second of motion, the moment people wait for |
| 0:07 to 0:15 | Cassette, waveform, artist and title on yellow | The payoff frame, and rewatches count |

The app never appears in a video. No interface, no link, no download. A clip that ends on an
unknown web address reads as a scam and gets scrolled past. The channel answers a question,
that is all it does, and the app lives in the bio for anyone who wonders how.

No voiceover. Text on screen only, TikTok's own font, white on black bar. Never use the
robot voice, it reads as low effort.

The yellow band with the turning cassette is the signature. Same position, same colour, same
motion in every single video. After ten posts people recognise the channel before they read a
word, and that is worth more than any logo in the corner.

## First ten videos

Pick sounds that are peaking right now, not sounds you personally like. The whole point is
that the viewer already heard it and never learned the name.

1. A sped up remix nobody can name, the original is the payoff
2. A slowed and reverbed edit, show that the tool finds the original release
3. A phonk track from a car edit
4. A song where the TikTok label is wrong, show the label, then the real name
5. A sound with an Arabic or Turkish original label, the tool reads through it
6. A clip where the song only plays under a voiceover
7. A movie scene edit with a track underneath
8. The same song in three different edits, one search each
9. A song that the comments guessed wrong, show the wrong guess first
10. Your own collection scrolling, forty tracks, all found this way

## Rules that keep the account alive

**Do not repost other people's videos.** Use Stitch or Duet for the clip part. That is what
TikTok built them for, credit is automatic, and reposts get the account limited fast.

**Never say download.** Say find the song, identify, get the name. The word download attracts
the wrong attention and the wrong audience.

**One call to action, at the end, in text.** No link spam in comments, no follow for part two.

**Post three to five times a week for three weeks before judging anything.** TikTok needs
volume to place a new account. Fewer than twenty videos tells you nothing.

## What to measure

Completion rate first, average watch time second, everything else later. If people leave
before the payoff at 0:06, the hook is wrong. If they leave after it, the search took too long
on screen, cut the waiting. Rewatches and shares weigh more than likes.
